# Requirements Clarification

## Purpose

Make the needs for the service change precise enough to guide solution choices.

## Outcomes

- The needs have observable acceptance conditions.
- Material ambiguities and conflicts are resolved or identified with their consequences for solution choice.

## Activities & Tasks

### Understanding the needs

1. Identify the needs, concerns, and constraints of the change from stakeholder information and improvement proposals.
2. When a change brief already exists, carry over its content and distinguish what has changed.

### Clarifying acceptance conditions

1. Give each need an observable acceptance condition.
2. Record ambiguities and conflicts that cannot be resolved, with their consequences for solution choice.

## Inputs

Stakeholder information, improvement proposals, and any existing change brief.

## Outputs

The change brief.
