# Solution Design

## Purpose

Make the chosen solution concrete as a configuration that implementation and checking can use.

## Outcomes

- Each design component can be traced to the needs in the change brief.
- Design decisions that affect implementation and checking are identified with their rationale.

## Activities & Tasks

1. Identify the necessary components from the needs and acceptance conditions in the change brief.
2. Map each component to the needs it satisfies.
3. Record the design decisions that affect implementation and checking, with their alternatives and rationale.

## Inputs

The change brief.

## Outputs

The design description: a directory that gathers the components and design decisions.
