# Exhaust the Design Space

## Purpose

Base the commitment to a design on a side-by-side comparison of competing prototypes.

## Outcomes

- The commitment follows a side-by-side comparison of 2-3 competing prototypes.

## Tasks

The following Tasks are required whenever this principle applies, each with the conditions it states. Numbers identify the Tasks and set no order.

1. Build 2-3 competing prototypes and compare side by side before committing.

## Constraints

This principle applies when facing a novel UI interaction or architectural decision with no precedent in the codebase. When another description cites this principle, that description's own limits on it hold.

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.
