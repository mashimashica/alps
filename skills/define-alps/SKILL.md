# ALPS Definition Process

## Purpose

This Process establishes an assessable and usable ALPS representation that satisfies an identified need while preserving the semantics of the represented Process Framework construct.

## Outcomes

Success of this Process establishes the following conditions.

- a) The need for the ALPS representation and the intended contexts of use are identified.
- b) The represented Process Framework construct, Purpose, boundary, and required level of detail are aligned with the selected need.
- c) The authoritative representation satisfies the applicable Process Framework and ALPS requirements for its representation kind.
- d) References, relationships, provenance, and exchanges required by the representation are traceable and resolvable.
- e) A Process Description has demonstrated Outcome achievability in representative contexts, or a non-Process representation has demonstrated semantic consistency and applicability to its intended concern.
- f) A decision on adoption can be made from verification evidence that includes defects, assumptions, and limitations.

## Activities & Tasks

### Need and Boundary Framing

1. The representation need and its intended users, stakeholders, contexts, and risks must be identified.
2. Existing ALPS representations must be investigated for reuse, overlap, adjacency, and gaps.
3. The Process Framework construct to represent must be selected according to the meaning of the need.
4. The Purpose, boundary, and required level of detail must be determined.
5. The selection rationale and unresolved matters must be recorded.

### Representation Definition

1. One authoritative representation must be created.
2. Optional elements must be added only when their omission would lose Outcome evaluation, boundary, handoff, a material decision, or claim evidence.
3. References, relationships, provenance, and handoffs required by the representation must be defined.
4. Discovery information must be aligned with the authoritative content.
5. The roles and conditions of use of accompanying resources must be identified.

### Representation Verification

1. The representation must be checked against the kind-specific Process Framework and ALPS requirements and for internal consistency.
2. Mandatory references, required semantic-center equality, and required provenance must be checked.
3. Outcome achievability must be evaluated for a Process Description, or applicability must be evaluated for a non-Process representation.
4. Defects, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved matters must be recorded.
5. Verification evidence usable for an adoption decision must be produced.

| --- | --- |
| Need and Boundary Framing | a), b) |
| Representation Definition | b), c), d) |
| Representation Verification | c), d), e), f) |

## Inputs

- A representation need, concern, composition requirement, lesson, or change request.
- Intended users, stakeholders, contexts, and relevant risk information.
- Existing representations, source material, and available execution evidence.

## Outputs

- A verified authoritative ALPS representation.
- Selection rationale, required reference and handoff information, and unresolved matters.
- Verification evidence, including defects, assumptions, and limitations.

## Entry Criteria

- A need or change request for an ALPS representation is available.
- Intended users, stakeholders, and contexts can be identified.
- The applicable normative sources and existing representations can be consulted.

## Exit Criteria

- The representation kind, Purpose, boundary, and required detail are explicit.
- The authoritative representation and its required references, relationships, provenance, handoffs, and accompanying resources have been verified.
- Process Outcome achievability or non-Process applicability has been evaluated.
- Verification evidence is available for an adoption decision; adoption or publication has not been implied.

## Enablers

- Expertise in the represented domain and applicable representation kind.
- Capabilities for reference resolution, representative trials, and semantic comparison.

## Normative Basis

The [Process Framework](../../spec/process-framework.md) governs Process semantics, and the [ALPS Specification](../../spec/ALPS-SPEC.md) governs Agent Skill representations and the ALPS-specific requirements applied by this Process.

## Common Approach

- Select the Process Framework construct before choosing a physical form.
- Add optional detail only when the declared need, risk, handoff, decision, or claim justifies it.
- Use representative trials for a Process Description and semantic assessment for a non-Process representation.
