# Reusable Work Design Process

## Purpose

This Process establishes recurring or shared agent work as a reusable and
assessable Process Skill.

Creation and revision of the Process Skill representation are within this
Process boundary. Execution of the represented Process and governance of an
adopted Skill are outside it.

## Outcomes

- a) The need for reusable work and its intended contexts of use are identified.
- b) The represented work has one coherent Process boundary.
- c) The Process Name, Purpose, and Outcomes form one internally consistent
  semantic center.
- d) Every Outcome states one positive, observable, and assessable result
  condition relevant to the Purpose.
- e) The detail in the Process Description is proportionate to its intended
  discovery, application, composition, and assessment needs.
- f) Necessary references and handoffs are explicit and usable.
- g) The resulting Process Skill can be applied without relying on an unstated
  performer, tool, method, metric, or execution sequence.
- h) Material defects, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved matters are
  explicit.

## Activities & Tasks

The Activities, Tasks, and their order organize the description and do not
prescribe an execution sequence.

### Need and Boundary Design

1. The recurring or shared work and its intended contexts of use must be
   identified.
2. Existing Skills should be examined for suitable coverage before a new Skill
   is created.
3. One coherent Process boundary must be established around the related work.
4. Independent Purposes must be separated into distinct Processes.
5. The Process Name must be written as a concise noun phrase that expresses the
   central concern and does not summarize the Purpose.
6. An English Process Name should end with the word “Process”.
7. A project, product, or package brand must not replace the Process Name unless
   that brand is itself the central concern of the represented work.

### Semantic Core Design

1. The Purpose must be written as a concise statement of the related high-level
   objective for performing the Process.
2. The Purpose must encompass the expected Outcomes without summarizing the
   Activities or Outcomes.
3. Each Outcome must be written as one positive, observable, and assessable
   result condition.
4. An artifact, document, record, or information item must be classified as an
   Output rather than an Outcome when its production is the primary meaning.
5. The Outcome set must be checked for sufficiency for the Purpose.
6. Each Outcome must be checked for relevance to the Purpose.
7. The Name, Purpose, and Outcomes must be checked for internal consistency.

### Necessary Detail Selection

1. The need for each optional Process element must be evaluated against the
   intended discovery, application, composition, and assessment contexts.
2. Activities and Tasks should be added when their absence would materially
   reduce the reliability or consistency of application.
3. Inputs and Outputs should be added when transformation or exchange boundaries
   need to be understood.
4. Entry Criteria and Exit Criteria should be added when invocation or
   completion would otherwise be ambiguous.
5. Controls and Constraints must be added when they materially direct or limit
   permitted execution.
6. Agents, people, models, tools, and execution environments used to perform the
   Process must be classified as Enablers rather than Inputs.
7. An execution sequence must be stated only when a genuine temporal Constraint
   requires it.
8. When Process composition is in scope, every provider Output to recipient
   Input mapping must be made explicit.

### Verification and Subtraction

1. Each statement must be tested by determining whether its removal would
   materially reduce correct discovery, application, composition, or assessment.
2. A statement that does not pass that test must be removed or moved to
   reference information.
3. The Skill discovery description must be checked for consistency with the
   Process Name, Purpose, Outcomes, boundary, and applicable conditions.
4. Mandatory references must be resolved.
5. Representative trials should be performed when uncertainty or quality risk
   makes Outcome achievability unclear.
6. Detected defects, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved matters must be
   recorded.
7. A perspective independent of the author should be obtained when the quality
   risk warrants it.

## Inputs

Representative Inputs include:

- a recurring or shared body of agent work;
- an existing Process Skill requiring review or revision;
- intended users and contexts of use;
- applicable Controls and Constraints; and
- execution evidence or lessons relevant to a revision.

## Outputs

Representative Outputs include:

- one authoritative Process Skill representation; and
- verification findings when they are needed for a subsequent decision.

## Entry Criteria

- A recurring or shared work need, or a request to review or revise an existing
  Process Skill, has been identified.
- The intended contexts of use can be identified sufficiently to establish the
  Process boundary.

## Exit Criteria

- Achievement of every declared Outcome has been assessed.
- The authoritative Process Skill representation is available.
- Mandatory references resolve.
- Material defects, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved matters are
  explicit.
- The representation can be transferred for a separate adoption decision.

## Controls

- The [Process Framework](../../spec/process-framework.md) governs Process
  semantics.
- The [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) governs
  the physical Skill form.
- Applicable system, user, repository, safety, privacy, and legal instructions
  govern the work within their declared scope.

## Constraints

- One-off work must not be converted into a Process Skill unless a concrete
  reuse, sharing, composition, review, or assessment need exists.
- A general Process Description must not prescribe a particular performer,
  method, tool, metric, management method, or execution sequence.
- This Process must not be expanded to execute the Process being described or
  to govern an adopted Skill asset.
- Completion of this Process must not be treated as independent proof of the
  resulting representation’s Conformance.
