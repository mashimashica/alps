# ALPS Application Process

## Purpose

This Process selects and activates applicable ALPS representations, resolves the Processes needed for the application situation, and achieves the intended Outcomes through the single or combined application of Process Skills.

## Outcomes

Success of this Process establishes the following conditions.

- a) The needs, conditions, and risks of the application situation are identified.
- b) Applicable Process Models, Process Reference Models, Process Views, and Process representations are selected or activated as needed.
- c) Candidate Processes are resolved from the selected or activated representations.
- d) The Process Skills to invoke and the form of application are determined with a rationale.
- e) Applicable Controls, Constraints, Tailoring decisions, and Decision Gates are identified before affected actions occur.
- f) Process Instances are executed within the declared application scope and applicable Controls, Constraints, and Tailoring decisions.
- g) The declared Outcomes of the applied Processes are achieved with observable evidence.
- h) Required handoffs are established.
- i) When multiple Processes are composed, completeness within the selected composition scope is established.
- j) Consistency across applicable Process relationships and structural levels is established.

## Activities & Tasks

### Representation Selection

1. The application need, target Outcomes, context, and risks must be identified.
2. The representation kind of each candidate must be confirmed.
3. Process Models, Process Reference Models, and Process Views must be activated only when needed for selection.
4. Candidate Processes must be selected by resolving the applicable logical references.
5. The selected Processes, form of application, and selection rationale must be recorded.

### Process Invocation

1. Invocation readiness must be confirmed from the selected Process representation and its Entry Criteria.
2. Required Inputs, Enablers, Controls, Constraints, Tailoring decisions, and Decision Gates must be identified.
3. The Process must be executed within the declared scope without assuming a sequence not established by a Constraint.
4. Exit Criteria and Outcome evidence must be evaluated before completion is declared.
5. Decisions, deviations, limitations, and unresolved matters must be recorded.

### Process Composition

1. The target Outcomes for the composition must be identified.
2. Every provider Output to recipient Input mapping must be made explicit.
3. The meaning, scope, and quality conditions of each exchanged item must be aligned.
4. The effects of changed Outputs must be propagated to recipient Inputs and applicable criteria.
5. Completeness, consistency, handoffs, and lessons must be evaluated for handoff.

| --- | --- |
| Representation Selection | a), b), c), d) |
| Process Invocation | d), e), f), g) |
| Process Composition | g), h), i), j) |

## Inputs

- The application need, context, target Outcomes, and risk information.
- Managed representations, their status and application conditions, and applicable Tailoring decisions.
- Process Descriptions and the declared Inputs for Processes selected for Invocation.

## Outputs

- Selected Processes, the form of application, and selection rationale.
- Process Outputs, execution and Outcome evidence, and recorded decisions or limitations.
- Explicit handoff mappings, composition assessment, and lessons.
- Unmet representation needs or change requests.

## Entry Criteria

- The application need and material risks can be identified.
- Candidate representations, or the absence of candidates, can be identified.
- Priority Controls and critical Constraints can be determined.

## Exit Criteria

- Representation selection and Process Invocation decisions are recorded.
- Entry and Exit Criteria and Outcome evidence for each invoked Process have been evaluated.
- Required handoffs and, when applicable, composition completeness and consistency have been evaluated.
- Unresolved needs, change requests, limitations, and lessons are available for the applicable handoff.

## Enablers

- Access to managed ALPS representations and capabilities for logical-reference resolution and selected Process execution.

## Normative Basis

The [Process Framework](../../spec/process-framework.md) governs Process semantics, and the [ALPS Specification](../../spec/ALPS-SPEC.md) governs representation activation, Process Invocation, composition, and the ALPS-specific requirements applied by this Process.

## Common Approach

- Activate Models or Views only when they improve Process selection or composition.
- Keep representation activation distinct from Process Invocation.
