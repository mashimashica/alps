# Fenwick measurement notes — snapshot 2026-08-31, 09:00 UTC

All records are synthetic. Shipment dates are UTC. Standard orders have one unit and a standard accessory set; complex orders have multiple units or a customer-specific accessory configuration. These definitions did not change.

`shipment_cohorts.csv` contains mutually exclusive date/line/order-band cohorts. `shipped_orders` includes every dispatched order. `mature_orders` counts orders whose complete seven-calendar-day customer observation window has elapsed. Cohorts mature together, every mature order has a deliverable customer address, and there was no selective exclusion. `confirmed_mispack_7d` counts distinct mature orders with a customer-confirmed wrong or missing packed item reported within that window. Multiple issues on an order count once. Equipment repair faults are excluded. Customer reporting and this outcome definition are stable across lines and periods.

August 28 cohorts are immature: zero outcome counts accompany zero eligible orders and do not establish error-free shipping or even an absence of early reports. `station_catches` describes pre-dispatch package-content issues. Before August 14, staff recorded only catches requiring supervisor assistance. From August 14, both lines had to scan every catch. These counts are not consistently captured before/after outcomes and must not be added to downstream mispacks.

`shift_operations.csv` has one row for the whole line on each date, not one per order band. `productive_labor_hours` includes all paid time assigned to the shipments, including overtime and identified training, and excludes lunch and unrelated maintenance. `overtime_hours` and `training_hours` are subsets of the productive total. Only Alder's five hours on August 17 are one-off trial training; routine checker work is already included. Hours must be counted once per date and line even when analyzing two shipment bands.

`late_dispatch_orders` counts distinct shipped orders missing the promised carrier loading cutoff. It is immediately observable, including for immature cohorts; use shipped orders for its denominator. August 3–5 and August 17–19 were ordinary full shifts with the same crews and no absences, holidays, or outages. August 28 was a shorter 600-order shift with an unusually simple mix. It is included as the latest available record, not as evidence of full-shift capacity. There are no missing rows or recorded minutes per band/check.
