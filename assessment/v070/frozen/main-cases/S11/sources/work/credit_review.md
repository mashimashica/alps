# Review rental-charge relief and warranty requests

The finance reviewer handles rental-charge relief under C2 of the shared [customer-care rules](../policy/customer_care_rules.md). Verify the rental agreement, failed acceptance self-test, signed carrier proof of delivery, asset serial number, and first-reported date. Check the independent 0–14-calendar-day window from carrier-confirmed delivery. Directly owned units do not qualify for this rental benefit. Record the evidence and the authorized reviewer's decision; never promise an automatic adjustment.

For a warranty request, assemble purchase evidence, product terms, serial number, carrier-confirmed delivery date, and the diagnostician's disposition for the warranty owner. The C3 180-calendar-day assessment window remains delivery-based. Finance does not approve hardware warranty coverage on the diagnostician's behalf.

Keep the intake lane label with the case as context, but do not recompute or use Launch Assist eligibility as a commercial entitlement. A change in the lane label alone is not grounds to reopen a completed credit or warranty decision. Preserve safety restrictions until the safety lead clears them.
