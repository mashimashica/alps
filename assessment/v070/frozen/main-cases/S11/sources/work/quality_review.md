# Maintain the monthly early-failure quality series

The quality analyst prepares the fixed early-failure series defined in C5 of the shared [customer-care rules](../policy/customer_care_rules.md). This is a product-quality measure, not a support-lane workload measure.

Use the carrier-confirmed delivery date and earliest functional-failure report. Count failures reported on calendar days 0–14 after delivery, and include only shipments with a complete 14-day observation window in the denominator. Retain the established product-family scope and the distinction between rental and directly owned units in the underlying data. Check duplicate serial-number events and preserve an audit link to the source report.

The monthly figure must remain comparable with the historical delivery-based series. Do not replace its denominator with cases, use the commissioning date as its origin, or count Launch Assist labels as a proxy for qualifying failures. Keep support-lane volume in a separate operational measure when requested.

If a source correction changes a previously published quality count, follow the quality owner's existing correction process. A change to current diagnostic routing alone does not authorize historical restatement.
