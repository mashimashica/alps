# MFI-CARE-07 — Customer care rules

Revision: 6  
Effective: 2026-03-01  
Maintained by: Customer-care operations owner

## C1. Launch Assist

Launch Assist is available for directly owned Meridian bench sensors in the B-series. Rental units, fluid assemblies, and equipment installed by an unapproved third-party installer are excluded. A qualifying functional symptom must first be reported from day 0 through day 14, inclusive, after the carrier-confirmed delivery date. Use the delivery date from the signed carrier proof of delivery for the asset serial number. Missing or disputed delivery evidence goes to the care operations lead; use ordinary diagnostic booking while eligibility is unresolved.

Eligible cases receive the first available senior diagnostician appointment within one business day of classification. Launch Assist changes diagnostic scheduling priority only. It does not itself authorize replacement, rental-charge relief, a refund, or warranty coverage. The C4 safety stop overrides this route.

## C2. Rental-charge relief

For a Meridian rental unit that arrives unable to complete its acceptance self-test, the rental-charge relief review is available when the failure is first reported from day 0 through day 14, inclusive, after carrier-confirmed delivery. The finance reviewer verifies the signed carrier proof of delivery, self-test record, and rental agreement. Only that reviewer may approve the adjustment. The review is not available for a directly owned unit, and a support-lane label is not evidence of entitlement.

## C3. Warranty assessment

The standard hardware warranty assessment uses a 180-calendar-day window from the carrier-confirmed delivery date and the product's warranty terms. Obtain purchase evidence and the serial number. A diagnostician recommends a disposition; the warranty owner approves coverage. Scheduling priority neither extends the window nor establishes a covered fault.

## C4. Safety escalation

If a caller reports a hot, swollen, leaking, or smoking battery, stop remote diagnostic instructions and direct the case to the safety lead using the existing emergency handoff. Do not ask the customer to power-cycle, open, pack, or return the unit while that concern is unresolved. Scheduling and commercial assessments wait for the safety lead's direction. This rule applies regardless of ownership, product lane, or time since delivery.

## C5. Fixed early-failure quality series

The monthly quality series counts first functional failures reported from day 0 through day 14, inclusive, after carrier-confirmed delivery, divided by shipped units with a complete 14-day observation window. Its delivery-based definition is fixed for comparability with the historical series. Include both rental and directly owned eligible product families as specified in the quality data dictionary; diagnostic support priority is neither an inclusion criterion nor a substitute for the dates. Do not restate the series as the number of Launch Assist cases.
