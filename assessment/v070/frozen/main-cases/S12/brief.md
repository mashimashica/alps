# Reassess the support setup for Stonewake's receiving review

Stonewake Parts wants a reusable Agent Skill for reviewing whether an inbound parts lot is ready for an authorized receiving lead to release from quarantine. The team has an existing written process, but several cumbersome support instructions were introduced because its old evidence viewer could not join records, apply count corrections, or preserve row references. The local tool has now gained those capabilities.

Create the business Skill and a usable local support setup for it. Reassess the previous support notes in light of the actual supplied tool, retaining the receiving criteria and permission boundaries while changing any capability-dependent guidance that is no longer justified. Then use the resulting setup to perform a local review of the supplied fictional demonstration packet. Give the receiving lead a usable result and make the relevant evidence inspectable; choose a suitable form rather than following a prescribed report or checklist.

`sources/release_policy.md` is the business authority. `sources/prior_support_notes.md` records the former working arrangement and explains why its workarounds existed; it is not an immutable policy. `sources/tool_interface.md` announces the upgrade, its supported interface, and its limits. The actual tool is `tools/stock_evidence.py`, a small Python-standard-library command available in this environment. Inspect and use that capability rather than accepting or rejecting the upgrade solely on the announcement. The files in `demo/` are a nonproduction packet for exercising the local setup, not proof that every later packet will be well formed.

The new tool is an evidence processor, not a receiving authority. The Skill should remain valid for a later packet with different quantities, source problems, or quality context. The team does not want a new service, custom runtime, or duplicate matching implementation when the available command supplies the needed work. If some manual check or helper is still warranted, connect it to a remaining business or capability need. Make the final support instructions clear about their applicable tool capability and what to do if that capability is absent or fails.

You may read and run the supplied local tool and read the source packet. Create the Skill, revised supporting material, and demonstration-review deliverables under a new `deliverables/` directory. Leave `sources/`, `tools/`, and `demo/` unchanged. No external service is needed or authorized. Do not change inventory, release quarantine in a live system, contact suppliers, invent missing quality clearances, or treat a successful tool run as approval to act. Actual release remains the receiving lead's responsibility.
