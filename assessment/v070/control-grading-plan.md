# C-control grading allocation

The fixed C stage contains 72 generated packages and 144 applications. Every package and both applications receive a primary business assessment using grading-guidance.md and the independently frozen family oracle. C is assessed for the work's meaning and usefulness, without requiring ALPS headings or vocabulary. Physical format, package quality, requested-task adequacy, business result, satisfied conditions and evidenced compensation remain separate judgments.

Before any C application, control-grading-allocation.json fixes a shuffled allocation within each family into three four-package packets. Analysis seed 700 reproduces this allocation only, not model behavior. The first packet in every family also receives an independent second assessment regardless of primary results: 24 packages and 48 applications. Any material or critical failure, uncertain compensation count, consequential grading dispute, or ambiguous oracle finding outside that subset receives a targeted second assessment before closeout. A second reviewer receives the same original evidence without the first report. Root adjudicates against business evidence and preserves disagreement and uncertainty.

Creators and consumers remain separate fresh task contexts. Graders receive only assigned package and application evidence, raw original business inputs and oracle, common grading guidance, and optional frozen skill-creator. No experimental configuration mapping, development hypotheses, source proposals or other grade is supplied. Original content and public observation notes remain unchanged; embedded paths may reveal experimental provenance or stylistic hints. This is limited identity blinding, not a claim that every hint of the arm has been concealed. Graders must not follow those paths or infer source attribution from them.

Packaging occurs only after explicit completion notifications, preservation of the exact generated Skill, and final-state snapshots where needed. Original inputs are copied from their recorded source identities, not from creator-added files. S06 original fixtures and initial/final native SQL are grader evidence, never consumer inputs; no live database is supplied to graders. S10 original and final JSON states are kept separately. A missing state or incomplete public observation is reported as such, not recreated to obtain a result. Component probes use disposable copies, never the evaluated artifact or source state. They test components, not additional fresh consumer work, and are recorded separately.

Each primary report covers all four packages and eight applications in its packet, with a table and reasons for relevant description/configuration scores, task adequacy and business result. Evidence of compensating behavior must identify the erroneous instruction and actual correction; a normal business choice does not count. Creator-reported validation and independently performed checks must remain distinguishable. An efficient implementation, alternative sound wording or adequate conditional answer does not fail merely because it differs from an oracle illustration.

These descriptive C findings cannot qualify B, satisfy the main A/B numerical acceptance routes, reopen ordinary development, close the example pool, or establish release readiness. All 72 planned package slots and 144 application slots remain visible, including missing or invalid attempts. Report family/configuration counts and limitations without presenting correlated applications as independent generated packages.
