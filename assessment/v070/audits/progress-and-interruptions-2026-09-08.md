This work was necessary to avoid inventing observations or replaying successful trials, but the need for this much recovery also exposes inadequate preservation coverage before interruption.

The experiment's dependency design is a separate coordinator responsibility. Up to 1,776 pending task slots depend on a qualified B or the subsequent complete package. The coordinator also delayed answering status questions while continuing long work. A status question is steering, not cancellation: the correct response is a prompt factual update followed by continued authorized work. Reporting itself does not require ending the task. This reporting defect should not be attributed to the infrastructure.
