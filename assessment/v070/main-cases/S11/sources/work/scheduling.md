# Schedule diagnostic work

Scheduling assigns an appropriate appointment from the intake handoff under the shared [customer-care rules](../policy/customer_care_rules.md). It does not change the product eligibility policy or approve commercial remedies.

Before booking, confirm the case ID, serial number, selected lane, safety restriction, and contact details. A safety handoff under C4 takes precedence over any appointment target. For the Launch Assist slot worksheet, calculate days from the supplied delivery date to the first-reported date; reserve a senior diagnostician slot when that number is from 0 to 14 and the remaining C1 conditions are met. If the worksheet and intake's selected lane disagree, return the case to intake with the discrepancy rather than independently changing the source date.

Offer a qualifying Launch Assist case the first available senior diagnostician appointment within one business day of classification. Ordinary cases use the next available qualified diagnostician. If the promised Launch Assist capacity is unavailable, escalate to the scheduling lead before making a customer promise. Record the offered appointment, responsible diagnostician, and any capacity escalation in the case.

Pass product and symptom evidence to the diagnostician. Any replacement, rental-charge adjustment, or warranty approval follows its separate owner-controlled work. Do not treat priority scheduling as approval of a replacement shipment.
