# Intake and route a customer-care case

Intake turns a customer's initial fault report into a traceable case and a safe next handoff. Use the shared [customer-care rules](../policy/customer_care_rules.md), especially C1 and C4.

Collect the asset serial number, product family, ownership type, installer approval status, symptom, first-reported timestamp and site time zone, contact details, signed carrier proof of delivery, and delivery date. Keep a missing-evidence note when a required source is unavailable; do not manufacture a date.

Check the C4 safety stop before giving diagnostic instructions or selecting a booking lane. For a non-safety case, route a directly owned B-series bench sensor through Launch Assist if it is in its first 14 calendar days after delivery and otherwise meets C1. Excluded products and rentals follow ordinary diagnostic intake. Escalate disputed delivery evidence to the care operations lead rather than choosing the faster lane on an assumption.

Send scheduling the case ID, serial number, product and ownership details, selected lane, first-reported date, delivery date, proof-of-delivery reference, any safety restriction, and a missing-evidence note. The scheduler needs to be able to verify the routing basis. Preserve the original symptom report and timestamps; do not overwrite them when later evidence arrives.

If rental-charge relief or warranty is requested, send the relevant evidence to the corresponding reviewer. Do not promise either benefit from a Launch Assist label. Intake owns case completeness and the initial routing record, not the approval of commercial remedies.
