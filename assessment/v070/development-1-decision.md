repetition 1, A/dev1-001 and B1/dev1-002 both prescribe new qualification and both
consumers of each create an avoidable approval blockage. In repetition 2,

matched S10 pair showing the proposed benefit. B1/dev1-003 also contains a wrong
approval-field comparison, compensated by both consumers; A/dev1-004 does not.

The small diagnostic neither proves the note never helps nor estimates a
general effect. It is sufficient to reject progression under H01's prospective
rule. Do not carry the note forward just because its source semantics are valid.
This is an inconclusive improvement mechanism, not an established convergence
plateau or a PF defect. B1 and its original trials remain preserved.
