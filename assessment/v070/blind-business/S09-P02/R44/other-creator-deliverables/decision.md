These comparisons support a benefit hypothesis, not causal proof. East volunteered, had worse baseline complex performance and could regress toward typical performance. The common June 12 template could explain some improvement, and its independent effect is unknown. There are only three full shifts per period and 300 mature complex pilot orders per line; the one-error complex difference between pilot lines is fragile.
