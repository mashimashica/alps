# Operational intervention decision

Turn a local packet into an operating decision, not a dashboard. Read context and measurement notes before calculating. Treat the packet as authority for definitions, timing, costs, limits, and permissions.

## Workflow

1. Inventory objectives, hard limits, choices, workload forecast, costs, permissions, and horizon.
2. Validate unit of analysis, maturity, changed recording rules, incomplete cohorts, and join keys. Never double count a line-level measure when joining band rows.
3. Reproduce arithmetic with a local script or transparent worksheet. Report denominators and weighted rates by period, line, and material segment. Exclude immature outcomes; do not treat their zeroes as no errors.
4. Compare intervention and control evidence while naming confounding changes. Use process measures only when recording is comparable.
5. Translate plausible effects to next workload with explicit volume and mix assumptions. Separate observed facts, estimates, causal claims, labor, capacity, quality, dispatch, and unpriced consequences.
6. Make one usable recommendation. If evidence is insufficient, define a bounded, staffed, time-limited follow up with review date, stop conditions, and an interim state. Do not present an unmeasured variant as observed savings or capacity.

Provide a concise decision memo, reviewable arithmetic, local reproducibility command, uncertainties, and authorization boundaries. Treat short or immature shifts as context only. Training is a subset of productive hours; overtime is also a subset.
