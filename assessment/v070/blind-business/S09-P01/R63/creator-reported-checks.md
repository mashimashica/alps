## Key design choices

- The Skill is packet-independent: its instructions describe decision framing, maturity, stratification, confounding, capacity, cost, and decision gates without embedding Northbank's conclusion.
- Detailed formulas and the exact supported CSV contract are progressively disclosed in `references/decision-method.md`.
- A standard-library-only analyzer makes counts, denominators, mix adjustment, approximate sampling intervals, labor projection, and cost scenarios locally reproducible.
- The Northbank decision uses only mature seven-day outcomes. It excludes 800 June 26 shipments from downstream rates, does not combine station catches with downstream errors, and does not duplicate line-level labor across order bands.
- Rates are standardized to the next-period 80% standard / 20% complex mix because the observed order mix changed.
- June 1–3 and June 15–17 are used for ordinary full-shift labor and dispatch comparison. June 26 is excluded from that projection because the context identifies it as a short, non-ordinary shift.
- The recommendation ends all-order checking and blocks expansion. A five-shift complex-only validation is conditional on a five-shift no-check capacity and dispatch gate; the selective variant is not presented as proven.
- Unpriced dispatch effects remain operational metrics and are not included in dollar totals.

## Limits and checks not performed

- The quick validator checks Skill form and frontmatter, not business-decision quality.
- Approximate confidence intervals cover binomial sampling variation only. They do not account for non-random intervention assignment, common operational changes, clustering by shift, or other design bias.
- Planned-volume and selective-scope labor values are explicit proportional sensitivities, not observed 1,200-order capacity or observed selective-check workload.
- The analyzer supports its documented CSV contract; no alternate-schema packet was available for a portability test.
- No independent forward test or subagent review was performed because delegation was not authorized for this trial.
- No live staffing, line process, record, spending, communication, or manager decision was changed.
