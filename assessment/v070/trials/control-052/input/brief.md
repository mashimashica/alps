# Northbank dispatch verification decision

Northbank Refurbishment ships repaired household audio equipment from two packing lines. East has tried a second-person package verification step. The site manager needs to decide what to do for the next four-week operating period: continue the trial as it stands, change its scope, expand it, or stop it. A good recommendation can be conditional; it must be usable by the manager under the staffing and dispatch commitments that actually exist.

Create a reusable Agent Skill for making this kind of operational intervention decision from a local measurement packet and its operating context. Also use it to prepare the decision for this packet, including a local, inspectable way to reproduce the numerical basis. Use the tools already available in the environment; a new analysis script is optional, not a requirement. Choose an appropriate organization and presentation for the result rather than filling a prescribed report.

The supplied files are fictional internal records. `sources/shipment_cohorts.csv` and `sources/shift_operations.csv` contain the measurements. Their units, recording changes, and relationship are explained in `sources/measurement_notes.md`. `sources/decision_context.md` supplies the decision owner's objectives, limits, and launch context. Use that packet as the authority for this exercise; do not browse for substitute operating assumptions.

The manager wants an actual decision, not just a dashboard or a pass/fail against the headline error rate. Make the arithmetic reviewable, distinguish what the measurements establish from what they only suggest, and connect the recommendation to expected next-period work. Address any comparability, cost, capacity, or outcome-timing issue that can materially change the decision. A bounded follow-up may be useful, but specify what it would resolve and what happens in the meantime rather than deferring the whole decision.

You may read the supplied inputs, run local calculations, and create the Skill and associated local deliverables in a new `deliverables/` directory. Leave `sources/` unchanged. The Skill should remain useful when a later operator supplies a different packet; do not bake this packet's numerical conclusion into it. You have no authority to change production staffing, turn the intervention on or off, contact staff or customers, alter operational records, or commit spending. Do not perform external actions.
