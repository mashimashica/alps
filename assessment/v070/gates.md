# Assessment acceptance criteria

Fixed after calibration, including the independent gate review, and before any B1 or main creator outputs exist. B1's two-file source proposal has been frozen, but no B1 target Skill has been generated. These are experiment decision rules, not ALPS requirements or a certification system. Any correction to an invalid case is documented separately; thresholds are not relaxed in response to disappointing results. The adjudications below supersede the initial draft's ambiguous acceptance logic.

## Judgments and denominators

Use the calibrated 0–3 anchors for the four description and four configuration dimensions, retaining findings alongside scores. A score of 3 means adequate within examined scope, not universal correctness. Do not average away a material contradiction. C is not assessed against ALPS-specific presentation rules; source fidelity of A/B is a separate semantic assessment.

For each consumer, classify requested-task adequacy from the raw work request, supplied evidence and applicable conditions: adequate, bounded deficiency, material failure, or unconfirmed. This is a local assessment label, not a proposed fixed Process record. A task is adequate only when the requested achievable work is performed and reported faithfully, with no required correction remaining in the delivered work. A consumer may compensate for a defective Skill and still deliver adequate work; record that artifact defect and compensating effort separately. Correctly identifying an initially missing condition can be adequate handling; introducing an avoidable blockage is not. Intended business Outcome achievement is recorded separately, including deliberately failing rehearsals and cases with insufficient evidence.

The primary comparison is the mean fraction of adequate consumer cases per generated Skill, paired on family, creator configuration and repetition. Two consumers from one Skill are not two independent Skills. Report description/configuration findings, component faults, business Outcome achievement and requirement satisfaction separately. Invalid infrastructure attempts are retained, then rerun under a distinct attempt ID; unresolved missing observations remain unconfirmed, not silently dropped or counted successful.

Show the result by family, configuration and predeclared positive/incomplete/failure case type, not only the pooled average. Main families and the six creator configurations have equal weights; repetitions and the two consumer cases have equal weight within a cell. Use 10,000 paired hierarchical bootstrap replicates, resampling families and then matched repetitions within each family/configuration block while retaining both consumers together. Preserve the fixed configuration mixture. Use percentile 95% intervals and analysis seed 700; that seed reproduces analysis, not model output. Report the small number of family clusters and leave-one-family-out sensitivity. The declared empirical sampling model does not establish representativeness of all business work. Descriptive C comparisons do not substitute for paired A/B evidence.

An unknown business Outcome can accompany an adequate task response. An unconfirmed task observation instead means the evidence does not establish what the consumer did. Keep every planned slot and its weight. For unresolved observations, report completeness plus best-/worst-case adequacy bounds, and evaluate gates under both conservative and favorable A/B assignments. A gate is unconfirmed if these assignments can reverse it. Arm-blind infrastructure invalidity requires a recorded transport/runtime/resource-access failure unrelated to the generated solution; an incorrect call, dependency choice or output is not automatically infrastructure failure. Retry an invalid/lost task at most once, under a distinct attempt ID, using the first valid attempt rather than the best result. Retain all attempts and attribution uncertainty.

## Accepting a core candidate

Acceptance requires rules 1, 2 and 5, AND either the effectiveness route in rule 3 OR the burden route in rule 4:

1. The source-semantic scope lock is satisfied. No unresolved material defect in the shipped Skill, foundations, required references, EN/JA meaning, or native distribution is accepted. Applicable mechanical checks pass; unavailable native Host verification remains explicit and cannot be claimed passed.
2. Each edit has a reproduced problem and a stated mechanism. Targeted diagnostics must exercise that mechanism with fresh contexts, and affected cross-business regression must not reveal a new material failure attributable to the edit. Single lucky generations do not establish the mechanism.
3. In the full A/B comparison, the effectiveness route requires at least **5 percentage points** higher primary adequacy AND a paired 95% interval lower bound above **zero**. An interval crossing zero is inconclusive for this route, not evidence that no benefit exists. Even a positive interval does not prove the true gain is at least five points or establish general superiority beyond this evaluation scope.
4. The burden route requires a primary-adequacy 95% lower bound above **−5 points**, plus at least **20%** reduction in independently evidenced compensating interventions, with a 95% interval for reduction above zero. Count one distinct correction to an erroneous or unusable Skill instruction per consumer application, supported by an explicit execution note or observable divergence necessary to meet the raw contract; examples include replacing a nonworking invocation or bypassing an instruction that would invalidate an already satisfied condition. Repeated manifestations of one corrected instruction count once. Normal interpretation, requested business choices and necessary checks are not corrections. Use all A/B main applications with the same fixed family/configuration weights, not a result-selected affected subset. Two blind reviewers adjudicate every counted correction against source instructions and task evidence. Unknown compensation remains missing, not zero; apply missingness sensitivity as above. If A's mean count is zero, the relative-reduction route is unavailable. No measured user repair or extra downstream work may be concealed. Word/line/file counts and total tool-call counts do not qualify.
5. A reproducible new loss of a mandatory condition, false evidence claim, or material failure caused by the candidate blocks acceptance even if the aggregate improves. For a family showing at least **10 points** worse adequacy, inspect every discordant pair and require an independent mechanism check before acceptance. This is a trigger for examination, not an automatic claim of significance from small samples.

These sample sizes may yield inconclusive intervals. If neither acceptance route is established, preserve A and report the inconclusive proposed correction; an inconclusive effectiveness route does not veto an independently passing burden route. Do not relabel uncertainty as convergence or keep an unproven rewrite merely to open a core PR. The example and release assessment can still produce useful findings. A generated package defect in one model configuration does not automatically prove a source defect; its cause and replication matter.

The 5-point useful-gain target demands a change noticeable across many applications; the 5-point non-regression margin caps tolerated uncertainty on quality in the burden route. The 20% threshold requires appreciably less compensating work, and the 10-point family trigger catches concentrated regressions that pooling can conceal. These are prospective practical policy choices, not power estimates established by twelve calibration generations.

Burden computation uses reduction `1 - mean(B) / mean(A)`, with the same fixed weights and paired bootstrap as adequacy. A replicate with A = B = 0 contributes zero reduction; A = 0 and B > 0 contributes negative infinity, not a dropped sample. If the actual A mean is zero, the burden route is unavailable. A missing or ungradable public execution record makes that application's compensation count unknown, not zero; any such unresolved count makes the burden route unavailable rather than inventing an upper bound. Counts describe evidenced compensating behavior, not unobservable internal effort. These details were finalized after independent review, before any diagnostic output was inspected and before main collection.

Distinguish observed package/task defect, validity of its business/environment contract, replication of component behavior, and source attribution. Replaying a buggy generated script reproduces a package defect, not its source-level cause. Attribution to guidance requires a direct independently confirmed source contradiction or the stated mechanism plus fresh matched A/B evidence; inconclusive attribution stays explicit. Every observed valid-contract task failure remains in the effectiveness evidence regardless of whether source attribution is established.

## Candidate identities, stage order and trial accounting

Every proposed core version has an immutable copy, hashes and a patch. Complete bounded development and select one B version before main A/B starts; do not pool multiple B versions. Main data may reject that candidate but will not be used to quietly tune the same labeled comparison. Any further edit makes the consumed data developmental, requires a new version and fresh confirmation, and must fit the round/diagnostic limits below or be escalated. The old example stays fixed for the core comparison. Select and adapt the best eligible example afterward, evaluate its influence with core fixed, then freeze the complete package before final holdout, locale, transfer and release judgment. Holdout estimates concern that complete package, not the isolated core note.

The authorized protocol describes **up to 2,028 base task trials**, explicitly excluding bounded development diagnostics; it does not establish a user-imposed 2,028 all-inclusive spending ceiling. The independent review inferred such a ceiling from the review assignment, and that inference is not adopted. To make the additional scope finite, reserve at most **72 additional task executions** across three development rounds, infrastructure retries, or replacement confirmation after any consumed holdout. H01 reserves 8 creators and 16 consumers from that reserve. Report base and additional counts separately, including retries. Grading and mechanical checks are reported separately; a fresh creator/consumer is counted as a task even if called a diagnostic or Host check. Do not silently drop required base stages. If another required task cannot fit either budget or three rounds, stop that dependency and request a scope decision before exceeding the limit.

## Final package and example

The selected example must have no known unresolved material semantic, component, scope or evidence defect. All specified normal/failure/component checks and its independent unseen revalidation cases must pass their appropriate-task criteria. A business Outcome designed to remain unmet must be reported as unmet. Final EN/JA adaptation is a new artifact and is rechecked, not credited with the original winner's results.

Rank the eligible pool before unseen revalidation using independent pairwise review, in this order: demonstrated business adequacy across the examined conditions; clarity and completeness of the meaning-to-implementation/evidence correspondence; usability by a fresh reader, including traceable results and actionable failure information; and suitability as a compact, shareable illustration of both design responsibilities with actual bundled processing. Prefer no candidate merely for more files/tests or higher raw scores on an easier task. Explain ties with the above evidence; if still equivalent, use the lower preassigned trial ID as a deterministic tie-break, not an arm preference. Revalidate the top three once under the planned unseen cases, then choose the highest-ranked one that passes. If none passes, report no eligible replacement rather than repeatedly fishing. Preserve originals; adapted artifacts receive the planned example-influence and final-package checks, and any extra fresh task draws from the diagnostic reserve. The result is best within the assessed eligible pool, not globally best.

The final A/B holdout uses the same primary judgment and **−5-point** non-regression margin, with no new reproducible candidate-caused critical defect. Pooled results do not conceal locale, transfer or review-scope failures. A locale or consumer configuration with material discrepancies requires cause analysis and bounded correction/revalidation before claiming its support; native support cannot be inferred from Markdown links alone. Selection and review-only tests must show the intended boundaries, including no unrequested modifications, against their predeclared case criteria.

Before a release-readiness recommendation, all planned stages must be completed. A blocked final, semantic, example, or mandatory format/version/link/whitespace check prevents an unqualified overall recommendation. Review the complete task-owned diff, including added and removed files and distribution resources, before opening delivery PRs. A missing native Host session prevents claiming tested native behavior for that Host; static distribution checks can still support an explicitly limited compatibility assessment. Preserve existing Host adapters while reporting this limit, rather than deleting support to obtain a pass. No actual Release or merge occurs under this execution authorization.

## Convergence

At most three development rounds are allowed. A round records its reference candidate, hypotheses, edits, fresh diagnostics and affected regression. Two successive rounds without supported practically useful improvement establish only a bounded plateau among the examined changes. Wide intervals, unperformed work or rereading old evidence do not establish convergence. Development plateau and release gates are separate: only after independent final gates pass can both be reported together. If round three improves the candidate but two successive plateau rounds have not occurred, stop at the cap and report convergence unestablished. If a critical defect or unmet gate remains after round three, report the needed decision and scope impact. Any post-holdout correction consumes another round and fresh tasks within the limits; consumed holdout cases are never reused as independent confirmation. Do not modify fixed semantics or move criteria to obtain a pass.
