# Frozen public main-case bank

The twelve public creator tasks were frozen after the root read/review of all
three author groups and their applicable local capability checks, and before any
main creator assignment. The snapshot is `frozen/main-cases`; exact input hashes
are in `main-case-hashes.json` (36 files). S01, S05 and S10 preserve the calibrated
public business briefs. The other nine families preserve the independently
authored main packets. S08's availability-response prose was corrected to match
its supplied implementation before this freeze; development inputs remain intact.

These are raw creator inputs, not consumer answers or a catalogue of preferred
implementations. The old bundled ALPS example and the common frozen skill-creator
stay fixed during the main core comparison. New consumer packets and independent
business oracles are materialized only after associated creation has ended.

`prepare_main.py` packages the prospectively specified 288 A/B creators and 72
preselected C creators, using six declared model/effort configurations and two
repetitions. Its instructions keep the raw work scope and permit explicitly
requested local demonstrations for S11/S12. Common output paths do not prescribe
business prose or architecture. The helper compiles but has not executed the
official preparation: a frozen B identity and a documented bounded-development
selection are prerequisites. Preparing an assignment is not executing a trial.
