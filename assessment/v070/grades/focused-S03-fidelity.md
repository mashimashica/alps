## Material finding

**F1 — Documented uncertainty can substitute for the requested shared-understanding result (focus-001 and focus-002; high confidence).** Generated locations: [focus-001][G1], Purpose, Outcomes and Exit Criteria, plus “Incorporate replies and corrections” Task 3; [focus-002][G2], Purpose, Outcomes and Exit Criteria. The [original briefs][B1] request help establishing shared understanding and expressly reject a polished summary that lacks confirmation. The [Framework][F] §2 requires necessary, collectively sufficient result conditions and distinguishes an explanation from demonstrated recipient understanding; §§4 and 8 prevent Output completion or missing evidence from becoming success. The [design Skill][D], “Success and work description” Task 1 and “Description evaluation” Task 4, requires that correspondence and says identifying a defect does not satisfy the unmet condition.

In both packages, a source-grounded account with every material commitment still open can meet every listed Outcome and the Exit Criteria. Focus-001 additionally promises resolution in its Purpose without requiring any resolved shared understanding in its Outcomes. Focus-002 narrows its Purpose to visibility and correctability, so the defect is principally fidelity to the requested work, rather than simple internal contradiction. Their required conversational checks are valuable but do not repair the missing intended result condition. The consequence is that description-based evaluation could call the Process complete while participant understanding remains unestablished. This does **not** require the assistant to force agreement, invent replies, perform dispatch or continue indefinitely: a useful pause is valid, with the establishment result still unmet.

## Bounded limitations and presentation

**C1 — A capability substitutes for an achieved condition (focus-001; high confidence).** [Outcome 5][G1] says later replies or corrections “can revise” affected understanding. Under [Framework][F] §§1–2, capability language is distinct from an observable result established by this work. It does not directly test whether the current account actually incorporates applicable corrections. The mandatory revision Tasks substantially mitigate operational ambiguity, so this is a bounded assessment-wording limitation additional to F1.

**C2 — The original Lumen authority fact needs resupply in focus-001/002 (medium confidence; conditional applicability limit).** Their Inputs/Controls require use of supplied operational and authority facts, but neither [package][G1] [retains][G2] the brief's specific rule that incomplete dispatch requires Maya's explicit approval, nor references the brief at runtime. General authority handling is faithful. However, if a later Lumen application supplies only the quoted conversation, the package cannot itself recover that omitted rule; authority context must be obtained before the dependent judgment. This limits standalone application to that setting. It is not evidence that either Skill would actually authorize dispatch, and exact kit names or the example's sequence need not be hard-coded. The applicable source is the [brief's contextual constraint][B1] and [Framework][F] §§3–4 on scoped required conditions and necessary detail.

No presentation-only defect with a material consequence was identified. All four use the required Name, Purpose and Outcome structure, coherent Activity groupings and numbered Tasks with an explicit required scope. Informative text does not visibly weaken prohibitions. Each Outcome's lists of owners, times or conditions generally describe dimensions of one result, not independent results merely joined by punctuation. Optional scripts, architecture, Enablers sections and fixed artifacts are unnecessary for this short conversational task under [Framework][F] §3 and the [frozen skill-creator][C].

## Source use, claims and limits

Read in full: all four generated `SKILL.md` files (the complete packages), all four original `input/brief.md` files, all four execution notes, frozen [design-process-description][D], its [Process Framework][F], [minimal template][T] and [examples][E], and the frozen [skill-creator][C]. Examples were treated as informative within the Framework's subject. No target work was performed and no generated package was changed.

All four notes report using the frozen ALPS design Skill, Framework, template and examples. Focus-001/002 report using the frozen skill-creator and its quick validator; focus-003/004 explicitly report not using that aid and instead record narrow standard-library checks. Focus-001/003/004 report consulting the official Agent Skills specification directly; focus-002 reports no external fetch and reliance on a supplied common format note. The latter note, trial prompts and external specification were outside this review's permitted evidence and were not read. Reported authoring-source use is consequently not independently reconstructed from execution logs. Notes 003/004 disclose truncated combined source displays and focused follow-up reads; those notes alone do not establish exhaustive source consumption.

The notes correctly limit format, heading, string-presence and manual coverage checks: they are not fresh-assistant execution or participant evidence. Focus-001's claim that its Outcome design prevents a summary from substituting for shared understanding is stronger than the generated success conditions support (F1). No reviewed evidence establishes general reliability or actual shared understanding. No causal attribution of F1 to ALPS or skill-creator is supported: the reviewed guidance requires preserving intended results and explicitly warns against Output substitution.

A separate supporting work-system design is not the requested subject, and these packages introduce no processing tool or software interface requiring an architecture. Interpretation and evidence judgment remain conversational; participants supply acceptance and approval evidence. Creator component/format checks do not establish effectiveness of that whole interaction. Consumer cases/results, oracles, mappings, other grades, unrelated trials, target execution, real participant behavior and all-context correctness were unexamined. No consumer adequacy score follows from this review.
