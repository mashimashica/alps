Both consumers expressly describe the equal reference and the *approval record's own* later sequence, then proceed on that correct basis. The public record therefore supports behavioral compensation for the contradiction. It does not show an explicit admission of correcting a typo, how the consumer selected the correct rule, or any private repair effort. The later correct control within R17 offers a possible basis for that resolution; no unseen source read is credited.

Neither R17's nor R28's omission of a new qualification is an extra correction: their qualification instructions are conditional, and the requests did not require replacement of valid current evidence. R28's relevant behavior is ordinary faithful execution. R44/R63's post-qualification stops follow their packages; they are not corrective actions. Their underlying algorithmic defect is package-attributable, while describing an unnecessary fresh qualification as “required” is also a user-facing application error. The unchanged tool correctly protects the gate; there is no evidence of an environment failure causing these blocks.
