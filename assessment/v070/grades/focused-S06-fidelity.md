A recovery-interface ambiguity in focus-006 and bounded semantic, role-classification, and implementation-condition gaps remain. These are fidelity judgments, not consumer adequacy scores or findings that a business application succeeded.

**Findings**

1. **Bounded recovery-interface ambiguity — focus-006, lost successful response.** [SKILL, “Interpret the processor result,” Task 4][k6] describes exit 21 when a page response cannot safely be incorporated, with quota-aware replay. In [the script][s6], `run` parses a successful page's stdout inside the `source_contract_violation` handler: an exit-0 page with empty or truncated JSON raises ordinary `RollupError` and exits **20**, not 21 (`parse_object`, `run`, `main`). The [note][n6] distinguishes successful unusable content (contract violation) from unavailable/ambiguous responses (exit 21), but does not make the lost-success boundary clear to the Skill user. The [brief][b6] expressly includes a lost response after a counted call; [design-principles §§4–5][principles] require clear failure distinctions and effect-aware retry guidance. This case receives the Skill's generic “correct the reported condition” instruction rather than its explicit uncertainty/replay instruction. The error does disclose `page_call_consumed: true`; the unchanged checkpoint and subsequent metadata check still protect replay. Thus an interface ambiguity is established, while a material recovery failure or incorrect total is not. **Confidence: high for the static classification; downstream agent response was not examined.**

2. **Bounded semantic clarity — coverage guards are not achieved coverage.** [focus-005 Outcome 1][k5] and [focus-008 Outcome 1][k8] qualify examination by “before” presenting/calling a result final or complete. [PF §2][pf] requires an established observable result condition, and the brief requires every entry to be examined even if future approval never arrives. A truthful paused, no-match request can satisfy a no-premature-claim guard while exhaustive examination remains unmet. Their Purposes, traversal rules and completion checks preserve the actual obligation, so this is not an authorization to substitute a progress report for the rollup. Assessment must treat coverage as unmet during the pause. **Confidence: high.**

3. **Bounded semantic clarity — independently assessable results share Outcome bullets.** [focus-005 Outcome 3][k5] joins identity and ordering; [focus-006 Outcome 3][k6] joins identity and completion distinction; [focus-007 Outcome 3][k7] joins identity, ordering and nonpositive-net retention; [focus-008 Outcome 3][k8] joins retention and ordering. [PF §2][pf] requires independent result conditions to remain separate Outcomes. Correct source identity can coexist with incorrect order, for example, so a single achievement judgment for these bullets can hide which condition remains unmet. The conditions themselves remain visible and their combined coverage is relevant and sufficient for the requested result; this finding concerns independent assessment, not missing vendor arithmetic or an invented requirement for more headings. **Confidence: high.**

4. **Bounded boundary-role defect — focus-005 classifies capabilities as Inputs.** [SKILL, “Inputs”][k5] lists Python and a local shell, and combines the API program with the source-state database. [PF §4][pf] classifies tools/environments used to perform work as Enablers; the ledger information is the source material being acted on. [AW, “Configuring responsibilities and interactions,” Task 3][aw] requires those roles to be related to the work. This classification obscures the distinction between request/source information and execution capability, although the actual invocation and agent/script allocation are clear. **Confidence: high.**

5. **Bounded operational-condition disclosure gap — focus-005 and focus-006.** Their [packaged][k5] [instructions][k6] specify Python 3.12 but do not disclose the `fcntl.flock` and filesystem semantics used by their [processors][s5] [for locking and durable replacement][s6]. [Design-principles §§4–5][principles] and [AW realization Tasks 1 and 3][aw] require necessary dependencies and execution conditions to be available to users. The focus-005 note identifies Linux/POSIX portability as untested, but that qualification is outside its reusable package. If the required locking facility is unavailable, import fails before the documented structured-result handling. This limits reliance in another Python/shell environment; it is not evidence of failure in the supplied environment. **Confidence: high.**

6. **Presentation only — focus-005 path count.** [Interface, “Invocation”][r5] says “All four paths”; the command has three path arguments (`--api`, `--source-state`, `--checkpoint`). The subsequent definitions identify them correctly. This conflicts with the applicable interface-clarity guidance but does not change permitted traversal, arithmetic, or completion meaning. **Confidence: high.**

The work detail otherwise contributes coherently to the Outcomes: input validation precedes dependent source calls; traversal and checkpoint commits establish coverage and continuation; interpretation/presentation makes incomplete or failed work visible. Imperatives, explicit required-Task language where used, and prohibitions retain the brief's normative force. The chosen scripts are justified by stable traversal, calculations and incorporation tracking that the existing API does not supply. Local package references resolve; required source CLI/state dependencies are identified, and no authoring-source link is silently made a business runtime dependency.

Note [007][n7] reports AW/principles, PD/PF, skill-creator and its validator, with no initializer or metadata generator. Those external/common sources were not independently opened in this restricted review, so their physical-format compliance is not certified here. The validator's inspected scope is frontmatter/naming/scaffold checks, not semantic or business effectiveness evidence.

Creator-reported checks were not rerun, and their logs/harnesses outside the allowed notes were not inspected. The notes support statements about reported component/integration evidence, not proof that an intended agent will interpret and present every future result correctly. Claims of general runtime effectiveness, arbitrary-scale capacity, guaranteed future grants, or all-interruption recovery would be unsupported; no such general conclusion follows from this review. Unperformed whole-system evaluation remains unconfirmed rather than failed or waived. No consumer case/result, oracle, mapping, other grade, unrelated trial content, or original embedded external path was used; no target business work or repair was executed.

The identified defects conflict with explicit source requirements; the inspected evidence does not establish that ALPS guidance caused them. No source-version recommendation or cross-arm comparison is made.
