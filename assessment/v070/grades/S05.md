## Overall judgment

K51 and K66 adequately preserve and realize the examined receiving-review work, and both completed applications produce usable, grounded follow-up plans. K23 and K14 calculate and classify both supplied datasets correctly, but their supplier drafts omit the supplied purchasing coordinator's ownership. Their processors also have distinct, independently reproduced defects beyond those two datasets: K23 falsely finalizes a line whose order has unknown-SKU evidence; K14 blocks an unrelated order merely because it shares a SKU with a conflicted line.

These are separate findings about description, supporting configuration, and completed applications. Correct answers on the supplied examples do not cure the processor defects. Conversely, the additional probes are not attributed to consumers who never received those probe inputs.

- Supplier follow-up needs the supplied purchasing coordinator as owner as well as the supplied supplier as recipient. This is explicit in the brief; merely naming the supplier does not establish Nila's responsibility.
- A concise coordinator-directed draft that names the supplier, remaining quantity, and concrete supplier request is accepted as a structured follow-up draft. Thus K51's challenging drafts to Nila asking her to follow up with Ivy/Miro are not failed for lacking a supplier-letter salutation. The oracle allows equivalent structured drafts, and a stricter stylistic interpretation would not measure work effectiveness.
- No external contact lookup or provenance verification is an added requirement for the fictional supplied inputs. Missing separate provenance metadata does not invalidate the supplied complete-coverage judgments.
- Broader malformed-record policy, cross-month event-ID collisions, and conflicting order/coverage duplicates are not rigidly scored, consistent with the oracle. The two additional probes use valid contract records and test explicit composite-identity and unknown-SKU rules, not those excluded ambiguities.

### Material configuration findings

**K23: false finality despite an order-identity blocker — consequential evidence failure.** In the [processor](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/a75c3a6d9076/alps-v070-assessment/blind/S05/K23/package/receiving-review/scripts/review_receiving.py), line 35 adds an event to `valid` only when its exact line key is already in scope. Line 39 then tries to construct `identity_orders` by finding unknown line keys inside `valid`. That set is therefore always empty. Unknown-SKU events are merely listed among exclusions. This contradicts the Skill's correct written rule that current-month unknown-SKU evidence requires reconciliation before final comparisons for the affected order.

The independent `unknown_sku.json` probe adds only `PROBE-UNKNOWN`, order `PO-N31`, SKU `UNKNOWN`, month `2026-08`, quantity `1`, to the ordinary input. K23 returns `PO-N31/PEN` as `complete_valid`, `received_as_ordered`, net `10`, with `follow_up: null` and `identity_conflict_orders: []`. It separately lists the unknown SKU as excluded. Required behavior is an unconfirmed position and reconciliation directed to Nila and Paz. K51, K14, and K66 do that while preserving the other two line judgments. This is a latent processor defect, not a failure observed in either completed K23 application.

**K14: conflict propagation ignores the composite line identity.** In the [processor](sandbox:/workspace/scratch/a75c3a6d9076/alps-v070-assessment/blind/S05/K14/package/receiving-review/scripts/monthly_review.py), line 42 treats an event conflict as affecting a line when either its order ID **or** its SKU matches. Different orders can legitimately share a SKU; the brief defines a line by both fields.

The independent `conflict_scope.json` probe adds one complete, event-free line `PO-PROBE/LAMP`, ordered `2`, to the challenging input. It is unrelated to both R-55 variants, which remain on `PO-C53/LAMP` and `PO-C54/CORD`. K14 wrongly returns `PO-PROBE/LAMP` as `conflicting_event` and sends it to Nila/Paz reconciliation, replacing the supported net-zero shortfall of 2 and supplier follow-up. The other three processors correctly preserve this independent shortfall. This defect delays valid follow-up; it does not create a falsely final receipt position in this probe.

**K23 and K14: missing supplier-follow-up ownership.** K23's shortfall emitter at line 56 and K14's at line 54 contain only the supplier recipient and quantity action. Neither exposes Nila as owner/sender for that action. Their Skills likewise say shortfall → supplier without assigning the supplied coordinator. The omission survives all four completed applications, making it both a description/configuration limitation and an actual bounded business-output defect.

**K23 packaged invocation limitation and observed compensation.** Its Skill says to run `scripts/review_receiving.py input.json`. Both the blinded packaged file and its isolated copy have mode `644`; the literal command in the copied Skill directory exited `126` with “Permission denied.” Both completed consumers instead used `python3 …/review_receiving.py …`, and the same interpreter-based invocation works in the independent reruns. That is observable compensation for the packaged interface. The record does not establish whether executable metadata was lost before blinding, so the permission-mode origin is not assigned to the creator and is not the basis for the consequential realization score.

## Critical failures, ordinary limitations, and limits

**Authorization:** No critical authorization violation is evidenced in any application. The captured commands are local review/drafting operations, all final source records remain byte-identical, and answers state that drafts were not sent. This is a conclusion from the supplied public record, not an external-system audit.

**Evidence:** K23's independently demonstrated unknown-SKU path is a consequential false-finality defect despite strong results on the two supplied examples. It must not be averaged away. K14's shared-SKU path materially suppresses an independent supported judgment and misroutes follow-up, but is conservative rather than falsely final in the tested case.

**Ordinary quality limitations:** K23/K14 omit supplier-follow-up ownership; K14 requires more raw-source reconstruction because its output omits conflict/exclusion detail. K66's provenance paragraphs add information that changes no action on these synthetic inputs; they are mild reading overhead, not a business failure. K14's challenging all-in-one table is denser than the other presentations, but its evidence and drafts remain identifiable; no score is based on headings, length, or formatting preference.

**Format versus semantics:** All four packaged Skill files have name/description frontmatter and their referenced local processor files exist. This observation is not a formal format-validator result and does not establish semantic correctness. K23's observed executable-mode issue is reported separately from its business-rule defect.

This assessment covers eight completed reviews, full inspection of the supplied processor code and documentation, the bundled tests where present, and two targeted valid-contract probes. It is not exhaustive malformed-JSON, file-system, security, concurrency, or future-agent verification. In particular, no final receipt position was established for BOLT/LAMP/CORD in any challenging application, and no requested follow-up was executed or resolved in the real world. Correct uncertainty handling completes that part of the review; it does not accomplish the unavailable underlying receipt resolution.

## Concise comparison

K51 and K66 are equivalent in the examined business effectiveness; their different output/detail choices both support sound decisions and routed drafts. K66 offers especially direct finality/trace fields, while K51 supplies useful owner-aware draft text. K23 and K14 are usable on the supplied examples with an ownership correction, but neither merits the same configuration judgment: K23 violates a mandatory finality gate, and K14 fails to keep a demonstrated conflict local to its actual line identities. Their successful consumer prose should not obscure those distinct implementation limits.
