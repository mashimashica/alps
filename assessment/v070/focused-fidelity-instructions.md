# A meaning and source-fidelity review

Review the four assigned generated Skills against the original business brief and the relevant frozen ALPS design guidance and foundations. This review asks whether the intended meanings and required correspondence survived generation; it is separate from business application grading. It does not compare experimental arms or recommend a new source version.

Read the complete generated SKILL.md files and their necessary resources, the raw creator brief/material, the relevant frozen ALPS SKILL.md files and required foundations. Creator execution notes may establish what sources/checks the creator reports using; they are not evidence of end-to-end effectiveness. Do not read consumer cases, consumer results, oracles, experimental mappings, other grades or unrelated work. Do not edit files, execute target business work, delegate, or make external changes.

Return one concise Markdown report with a four-row traceability table and evidence-linked findings. Assess Purpose/Outcome relevance, individual result conditions and collective sufficiency, necessary work detail and Activity/Task relationships, normative force, boundary roles and required references. Where work-system design applies, assess shared work meaning, judgment/processing allocation, interfaces/information, applicable conditions and the distinction between component verification and whole-system effectiveness.

Apply the foundations within their respective subjects. Optional detail is judged by need and its defined meaning, not by the number of headings. A short task does not need scripts or a separate architecture. A truthful incomplete application would leave some business Outcomes unmet; that is not a reason to weaken the Process's intended results or redefine a status report as achieving them. Conversely, a review or assistance Process has its own requested result and must not be assessed as if physical business execution were authorized.

Identify material semantic/operational defects separately from presentation-only issues and bounded clarity limitations. For each finding give the generated location, applicable source/brief requirement, concrete consequence and confidence. Attribute a generated defect to source guidance only with evidence of that causal relationship. Do not infer general effectiveness or all-context correctness from a coherent description or a creator's checks.

Report the sources actually reviewed, actual ALPS/skill-creator use reported in the notes, unsupported claims, and unexamined scope. No consumer adequacy score follows from this review.
