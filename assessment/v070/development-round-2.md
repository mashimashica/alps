## Problem and mechanism

The completed calibration source-fidelity review finds that two of six ALPS-aided
packages convey operational criteria but omit a separately assessable Purpose
and Outcomes core. It distinguishes this from alternative section labels or
absence of work meaning. The development-1 source-fidelity reviewer also reports
this pattern across new low-effort generations; its final evidence is checked
before starting this round. Both foundations and the Process Description Design
Skill already specify that core. There is no evidence that PF's meanings are
wrong or need extending.

H02 makes the core directly explicit at the Agent Work System Design Task where
the target Skill is authored. The existing phrase “reflect the work” is replaced
by an instruction to describe its Name, Purpose and Outcomes in SKILL.md, with
necessary detail, judgment criteria, tool use and conditions in the description
and resources. All existing Framework writing rules and sibling Skill references
remain. This repeats no definitions and adds no new section contract. The
target remains the work, not the authoring activity or a tool manual. No change
is made to PF, work-system principles, Purpose/Outcomes of either design Skill,
the old example, templates, scripts, distribution or evaluation criteria.

The two English/Japanese files are based on A, without H01's unproven note.
An independent semantic/locale review precedes the first B2 creator. All original
B1 files, generated packages and results remain immutable evidence.

## Diagnostics and decision rule

Reserve eight fresh luna/low creators: S05 × A/B2 × two repetitions and S10 ×
A/B2 × two repetitions, followed by two fresh sol/high consumers per package.
This uses another 24 of the 72 additional executions, leaving 24 for a final
round or necessary invalid-attempt replacement. Common skill-creator, physical
format information, raw public task input and tools remain identical across arms.

Reuse the independently authored development consumer fixtures in new isolated
workspaces after each associated creator ends. These are repeated developmental
tasks, not new held-out families or independent proof of generalization. No
consumer answer, oracle, or previous output is provided to a creator or consumer.

The directional target is improved application of the declared semantic core:
purpose distinguishes the work's objective and boundaries; listed result states
are individually assessable and collectively cover that purpose; operational
details preserve the same work. Equivalent meaningful labels are accepted. An
extra heading or copied text alone is not a successful mechanism observation.
Blinded business grading remains separate from source-fidelity assessment.

B2 proceeds to the main comparison only if at least one fresh matched pair
shows the hypothesized substantive uptake improvement without a new material
loss attributable to the edit, the remaining pairs do not establish a contrary
uptake mechanism, and independent semantic/locale review confirms the scope
lock. Failing or inconclusive diagnostics are retained; they do not justify
claiming source-level benefit or opening an improvement PR. The fixed main
effectiveness/burden gates remain necessary for eventual core acceptance.
